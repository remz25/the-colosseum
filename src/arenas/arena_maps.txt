# COLOSSEUM arena maps (scripts/arenas.py -> build/ArenaMaps.event).
#
# Each arena map is a region cut from a vanilla FE8 chapter map (read from FE8_clean.gba at build
# time, so no Nintendo data is committed). The arena keeps that chapter's tileset, palette, tile
# config and tile animations; only the map layer is new. The cut map is stored in the chapter
# asset table slot ASSET (a vanilla story chapter's map, never loaded by COLOSSEUM).
#
# The gameplay data (name, weather, spawns, hazards, floor pools) is in src/arenas/arenas.c;
# ARENA numbers must match its table.
#
# ARENA  NAME        CHAPTER  X   Y   W   H   ASSET
0        grand       0x43     0   0   15  10  0xDB    # vanilla asset kept (the original arena)
1        forest      0x14     2   1   15  10  0x04
2        desert      0x0F     4   1   15  10  0x08
3        volcanic    0x12     7   4   15  10  0x0B
4        cathedral   0x15     4   14  15  10  0x15
5        royal       0x13     7   5   15  10  0x25
6        abyss       0x15     1   6   15  10  0x39
7        ruins       0x2E     3   6   15  10  0x4D
