THE COLOSSEUM v0.1.0 - RELEASE NOTES
Status: early playable prototype / development build

This is the first public build. It shows the core loop of the game:
3-vs-3 battles, permadeath, the shop, relics, skills, weapon fusion,
3-win rewards, Elite battles and 8 arenas with weather and hazards.
Bosses, the Legacy system, story and presentation are not in yet
(see KNOWN_ISSUES.txt).

What was checked for this release
- The patch was made from the clean USA ROM and applied back to it;
  the result is byte-identical to the built ROM. A wrong base ROM or
  a damaged patch is rejected.
- The patched ROM was played in mGBA 0.10.5: new run and team screen,
  shop and R help, equipping a relic, player attacks with battle
  animations, victory and autosave, the 3-win reward, an Elite battle
  in a second arena (Royal Arena), Resume Chapter from the title
  screen, and several fights in a row.
- The build's automated tests (run state, combat, units, roster,
  skills, weapons, shop, relics, arenas, progression) pass.

Feedback that helps most
- Is the first floor too easy or too hard?
- Do you run out of gold, or have too much?
- Which relics, skills or rewards do you never pick?
- Any crash, freeze or wrong text, with a screenshot.
