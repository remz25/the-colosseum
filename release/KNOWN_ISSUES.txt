THE COLOSSEUM v0.1.0 - KNOWN ISSUES AND MISSING FEATURES

This is an early playable prototype. Please report anything not
listed here, with a screenshot and what you did just before.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
MISSING (planned for later versions)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
- No bosses yet. The 10th fight of a floor is a stronger normal
  fight; the floor still advances afterwards.
- No final boss or ending: a run continues until it is lost.
- Elites use FE8's normal enemy AI (no Elite-specific tactics yet).
- No Legacy / Hall of Champions, run statistics or records.
- No story, character dialogue or custom death quotes.
- The title screen, music and animations are vanilla FE8.
- The planned Shard and extended Fusion system is not in this
  version. Fusion today upgrades along fixed lines (Iron > Steel >
  Silver, tome lines, a few special pairs).
- Arenas with new tilesets (Frozen, Swamp, Graveyard) are not in yet.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
KNOWN ISSUES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
- Characters say their vanilla FE8 death quotes when they fall.
- The title menu keeps FE8's 3 save slots. "New Game" is hidden
  when all 3 slots are used; erase one with "Erase Data".
- Balance numbers (gold, shop prices, EXP, enemy strength, relic and
  skill weights) are first guesses and will change.
- Not yet tested by playing through (the underlying logic is covered
  by automated tests):
    - the level-up stat choice after an enemy-phase level-up
    - using a promotion item during a battle
    - some relic effects in real battles (Golden Thread, Guardian's
      Crest, Fortress Heart, Mage's Ring, Sturdy Boots)
    - a Champion Elite fought to the end, and its weapon drop
- Save states made in an older build may not be compatible. Use the
  in-game save.
