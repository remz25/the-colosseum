THE COLOSSEUM - CHANGELOG

v0.1.0 (2026-09-28) - first public build, early playable prototype

Everything below is new in this build, compared with vanilla FE8.

Core loop
- Run-based structure: New Game starts a run with 3 random
  characters; one reusable arena chapter hosts every battle.
- 3-vs-3 battles, 20-turn limit with warnings on turns 15, 18, 19.
- Floors of 9 normal fights with an Elite after every 3 wins; the
  10th fight is a stronger normal fight for now (no bosses yet).
- Permadeath; the run ends when all deployed units fall. A lost run's
  save is invalidated.
- HP carries over between battles; Recover heals the team (3 per
  floor, costs gold).
- Autosave after every win replaces FE8's save screen.

Characters and growth
- Pool of 25: 15 FE8 characters + 10 original characters (FE-Repo
  portraits, custom battle palettes).
- Start at level 5, cap 30, no stat caps; pick 1 of 3 stats on every
  level-up; player EXP x1.5.
- Promotion at level 10+, keeping the level; FE8's branching choice.
- Recruitment through rewards and the shop; deploy up to 3.

Skills
- 1 personal skill + 3 slots; 48 player skills in 5 rarities with
  prerequisites; learn / replace menus.

Weapons
- No durability for weapons and staves; weapon EXP x3.
- Transfer between characters; Fusion along weapon lines; Elite
  weapons from Champions.

Economy, rewards and relics
- Gold per win; 8-entry fully random shop, +10% prices per purchase.
- 3-win reward: 1 of 3 (Recruit / Skill / Promotion / Heal / Gold).
- Enemy drops (gold, skills, relics).
- 15 relics; each run starts with 2 Common + 1 Rare.

Elites and arenas
- Elite battles: Champions (one strong enemy) and Elite Squads
  (Guardian / Mender / Reaper / Striker roles); the first Elite of a
  run is gentler.
- 8 arenas with weather (rain, sandstorm, ashfall, fog of war),
  hot-rock hazards and sacred healing tiles; hazard-aware enemy AI.

Interface
- Prepare menu between fights: Next fight / Shop / Recover / Deploy /
  Transfer / Fuse / Relics.
- R-button help on every Colosseum menu row; B works as Back.
