# The Colosseum (v0.1.0)

**An FE8 roguelike: short 3v3 arena fights, permadeath, skills, relics and weapon fusion.**
Built on the FE8 Skill System. **Early playable prototype**: the core loop works, and bosses, story and presentation are still to come.

[image: 01_title / 02_team / 04_battle]

## How a run works
- Start with **3 random characters** from a pool of 25: 15 FE8 characters and 10 originals with FE-Repo portraits.
- Fight **3v3 battles** with a **20-turn limit**. HP carries over between fights, and fallen characters are gone for good.
- Every 3 wins, pick **1 of 3 rewards** (a recruit, a skill, a promotion, healing or gold), then face an **Elite battle**: a Champion or a coordinated Elite Squad.
- Between fights, use the **Prepare** menu: a random 8-item shop (prices rise with every purchase), Recover, Deploy, Transfer, Fuse and Relics.

## Features in v0.1.0
- Level 5 start, level cap 30, **no stat caps**. **Pick 1 of 3 stats** on every level-up.
- **1 personal skill + 3 slots**, with 48 skills in 5 rarities.
- Weapons never break. **Fuse** two weapons into a better one, and win Elite weapons from Champions.
- **15 relics** with stat, damage and gold effects. Every run starts with 3.
- **8 arenas** with weather (rain, sandstorm, ashfall, fog of war), burning hot-rock tiles and healing seal tiles.
- Promotion at level 10+ keeps your level.
- Autosave after every win. Press **R for help** on any menu row.
